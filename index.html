Xiaoyun Yin

Human Systems Engineering

I study trust dynamics, team coordination, and resilient performance in human-autonomy teaming — particularly in distributed space operations. My work bridges agent-based modeling, neural network approaches, and empirical team research.

xiaoyun.yin@asu.edu Phoenix, AZ Google Scholar
01 — Research

Research Interests

Trust in Human-AI Teaming

Modeling how trust and distrust spread dynamically in distributed human-autonomy teams, using recurrent neural networks and entropy-based approaches.

Distributed Space Operations

Designing and evaluating scenarios for future space missions where humans and machines must coordinate across communication delays and constrained environments.

Agent-Based Modeling

Using simulation to understand emergent coordination patterns in distributed teams, bridging computational models with empirical team cognition research.

Resilient Team Performance

Observing and measuring how teams adapt under perturbation — particularly in high-stakes space-based missions using digital twin technologies.
